World Timeline

An interactive map of world history from 3000 BCE to today. Press play to watch empires rise and fall and borders shift, or scrub the timeline to any year. Over 5,000 wars, battles, treaties, revolutions, disasters and discoveries from Wikipedia and Wikidata appear on the map as they happen, and every territory and event opens a summary. Switch to a 3D globe at any time.

Jump to a moment: the Great Pyramid, the death of Alexander the Great, the fall of Rome, the Mongol Empire, the fall of Constantinople, Columbus in the Americas, the French Revolution, the First World War, the end of the Second World War, the end of the Soviet Union.
